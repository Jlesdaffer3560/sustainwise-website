What you'll actually learn

Eight units, one learning path

  1. 01

    ESG & CSR Basics

    The core vocabulary every ESG conversation starts with — stakeholders, materiality, CSR, shared value, and what the three letters actually mean.

  2. 02

    Climate & Environment

    Scope 1/2/3, net zero, and the environmental footprint every company now has to count and report.

  3. 03

    Human Rights & Supply Chains

    Value chains, forced labour, and the human side of every supply chain — plus CSDDD, the law that turns human-rights due diligence into a legal obligation.

  4. 04

    Governance & Anti-Corruption

    Corruption, whistleblowing, and how a board is supposed to keep a company honest.

  5. 05

    Reporting Standards

    CSRD, ESRS, GRI, and the alphabet soup behind every sustainability report you will ever read.

  6. 06

    Sustainable Finance

    SFDR, the EU Taxonomy, and how money gets labelled "sustainable" — or doesn't.

  7. 07

    Claims & Greenwashing

    What a company is legally allowed to say about its own sustainability credentials — and what crosses the line.

  8. 08

    Context & History

    The institutions, milestones, and history behind everything above.

  9. ★

    Expert Challenge

    30 very hard questions across every unit — thresholds, edge cases, the fine print. Unlocks once all eight units are done.
