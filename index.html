Sample questions

Three real questions from the app

Environmental · EmpCo

Which of these is a "generic environmental claim" that EmpCo bans unless the trader can show recognised excellent environmental performance relevant to it?

Answer: "Eco-friendly" — generic claims like this are banned under EmpCo unless the trader can demonstrate recognised excellent environmental performance relevant to the claim.

Governance · CSRD

What does "double materiality" actually require a company to report?

Answer: How sustainability issues affect the company, and how the company affects people and the planet — double materiality runs both directions.

Social · Forced Labour Regulation

A supply chain audit turns up credible indicators of forced labour. What has to happen before the product can be pulled from the EU market?

Answer: A competent authority must investigate and issue a final decision — an audit finding alone isn't enough.
