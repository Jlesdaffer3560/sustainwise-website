Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

SustainWise is developed by NOVARISQ Consulting, based in Brussels, Belgium. This page explains what happens to your data when you visit this website.

What this site collects

This is a static marketing page for the SustainWise app, with no forms — there is nothing to submit. If you email us directly using the contact link on this site, that message is handled like any other email, under normal email-provider terms, not by us as a data processor.

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages are read. It does not use cookies or any other persistent identifier, does not collect personal data beyond what's needed for an aggregate visit count, and does not track you across other websites.

Third-party services

This site loads web fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Loading a font causes your browser to make a request to Google's servers, which may log your IP address. See Google's privacy policy for details.

Page visits are counted using Cloudflare Web Analytics (static.cloudflareinsights.com), a cookie-less analytics service. See Cloudflare's Web Analytics privacy information for details on how it works.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you can ask to access, correct, or delete any personal data we hold about you. To do so, contact us at jordi.lesaffer@novarisq.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app and website evolve. Check back here for the latest version.
