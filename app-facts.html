Learning path

Eight units form one learning path, followed by an Expert Challenge.

  1. ESG & CSR Basics — The core vocabulary every ESG conversation starts with — stakeholders, materiality, CSR, shared value, and what the three letters actually mean.
  2. Climate & Environment — Scope 1/2/3, net zero, and the environmental footprint every company now has to count and report.
  3. Human Rights & Supply Chains — Value chains, forced labour, and the human side of every supply chain — plus CSDDD, the law that turns human-rights due diligence into a legal obligation.
  4. Governance & Anti-Corruption — Corruption, whistleblowing, and how a board is supposed to keep a company honest.
  5. Reporting Standards — CSRD, ESRS, GRI, and the alphabet soup behind every sustainability report you will ever read.
  6. Sustainable Finance — SFDR, the EU Taxonomy, and how money gets labelled "sustainable" — or doesn't.
  7. Claims & Greenwashing — What a company is legally allowed to say about its own sustainability credentials — and what crosses the line.
  8. Context & History — The institutions, milestones, and history behind everything above.

Expert Challenge — 30 very hard questions across every unit — thresholds, edge cases, the fine print. Unlocks once all eight units are done.

How a lesson works

  1. Flip each flashcard to reveal its definition, then rate yourself "Still learning" or "Got it".
  2. Answer the quiz: a wrong pick still shows the correct definition and why.
  3. Sort the confusable pairs, the terms people mix up most.
  4. Finish a lesson to grow your streak and XP. Modules unlock one at a time, in order.

The app is built around one short session a day, about 15 minutes.

Who it is for

  • Professionals who need to learn ESG concepts fast.
  • Teams building ESG awareness across departments.
  • Organisations that want to test knowledge on regulation, claims and risk. Tailored ESG training built on the same content can be discussed via jordi.lesaffer@novarisq.com.
